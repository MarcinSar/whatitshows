What each one shows

parser: the shared parser reads the month first

The report shows a day and a month swapped: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.” The tenant writes the day first, and the report came in by app, by email, or through a booking-v2 form, whose hint asks for DD/MM/YYYY. People typed the date their way. The shared parser read it month first. This is the bug #4471 names, and the queued fix is for it.

hint: booking-v3’s form asks for the month first

The report shows the same swap, so it sounds the same. It only comes through the booking form, at day-first tenants that run booking-v3. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people type the month first, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. A parser fix does not reach it. The hint, or the way the form reads it, has to follow the tenant’s locale.

one-day: the date moves by one day

The report shows no swap. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” “The invite arrived before I made the booking.” The day moves by one, or differs between time zones: a time-zone conversion of the date, not its order. Neither the parser fix nor the hint fix touches it. The desk already noted these reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json); the #4471 page files them under parser by tenant and footnotes them.

How the reports were split

  • One report counts once. The export comes in pages that overlap, so the same report can appear twice; it is counted by id.
  • The cause comes from the tenant (its locale and its booking form) and the channel, as causes.ts decides it. Not from the intake tag, which is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and not from the subject line, which is how everything ended up in #4471.
  • Reports the desk noted as a one-day move are their own problem, whichever tenant sent them.
  • Every report merged into #4471 lands in exactly one problem. The table checks that the counts add up to the ticket.

The counts come from the data at render time. When the desk merges more reports into #4471, or notes another one-day move, render the page again; the counts follow.
